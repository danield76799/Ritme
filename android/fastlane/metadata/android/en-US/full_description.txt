Ritme supports daily tracking of your social rhythm: mood, activities, medication, sleep, appointments, weight, and reports. Everything stays on your device, protected with a PIN and optional biometric unlock.

What Ritme does
- Morning and evening mood checks with chips for activities, energy, and sleep
- Medication planner with reminders on clock time or SRT time
- Appointments, crisis plan with key contacts, diary, and voortekeningen
- Statistics and reports to spot patterns
- Biometric authentication with PIN fallback

Privacy
No account required. Ritme stores data locally with Hive (Android) and SQLite (iOS). Notifications run on the device itself - no tracking.

Who it is for
Built for people who want to monitor their rhythm, including in guided care. Ritme is self-observation and replaces no medical advice - always consult your care team for medication and treatment questions.

At a glance
- Dashboard with average mood and sleep chart
- Medication overview with reminders
- Appointments calendar with alerts
- Weight page with goals
- Report export
